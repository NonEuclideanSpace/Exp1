The air feels thick, as if the room were submerged in a lukewarm sea, and every object breathes in slow synchrony with the ticking clock and the fluttering moth. Shadows stretch in impossible directions, defying the light, and the space feels alive, a labyrinth of moments and echoes, stitched together by the fragile logic of a world that is almost real.
