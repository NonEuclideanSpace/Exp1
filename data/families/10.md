# 衣服与线轴

## C10A · seed 0

Prompt: A framed photograph on a wall shows a woman in a blue dress holding a red thread spool in one hand. Both the fabric of the dress and the thread on the spool are clearly visible. Soft natural light, still-life photograph, clear view of the contact points.

Expected: 墙上照片里，女子穿蓝裙，拿红色线轴。

Behavior (Qwen3-VL blind): 照片内裙子　蓝色　｜　照片内线轴　红色
